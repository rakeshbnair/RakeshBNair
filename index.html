About

Biography

Dr. Rakesh Bhaskaran Nair is a Principal Investigator and Early Career Research Leader in the School of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering at Dublin City University, Ireland. His research addresses a single question: how materials degrade in extreme operating environments, and how that degradation can be resisted and detected before it causes failure. He pursues this from two directions, engineering coatings that protect components against degradation, and developing diagnostics that reveal it as it happens. He currently leads a Research Ireland ARC Hub project developing a metal powder quality intelligence platform for advanced manufacturing.

On the protection side, he designs thermal spray and cold spray coatings, using advanced material systems engineered to withstand erosion, corrosion, wear, and high-temperature oxidation in aerospace, wind-energy, and marine applications, supported by erosion and erosion-corrosion test rigs he has designed and built himself. On the detection side, he develops real-time diagnostics for laser powder bed fusion, including a custom infrared and RGB imaging system and spectral reflectance analysis that assess metal powder quality and oxidation as parts are built. Together these lines of work form a single capability: certifying the integrity of materials in real time, rather than only after they have failed in service.

Dr. Nair is Lead Guest Editor for npj Materials Degradation (Nature Portfolio) and Discover Surfaces (Springer Nature). He received his Ph.D. from Shiv Nadar University, India, in 2020, and held postdoctoral research positions at the University of Alberta and Concordia University in Canada before joining Dublin City University.
